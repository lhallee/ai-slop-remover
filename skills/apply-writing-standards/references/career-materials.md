# Career materials

Use this profile for cover letters, application answers, professional statements, CV summaries, and bios.

## Cover letter or application

1. State the role and the specific intersection that makes it relevant.
2. Explain why the organization is unusually suited to that work.
3. Organize the body around two or three evidence threads.
4. For each thread, connect capability, concrete proof, and contribution to the role.
5. Close with a direct expression of interest or availability.

Show fit through systems built, evaluations designed, work released, results measured, or responsibilities held. Include execution evidence alongside ideas. Prefer one strong metric with its evaluation condition to a list of uncontextualized achievements.

Avoid generic enthusiasm, repeated claims of fit, chronological resume recaps, exhaustive expertise lists, and the same accomplishment in multiple paragraphs.

## Bio

Use third person unless the user requests otherwise. Select only the role, expertise, and evidence relevant to the bio's venue and length. Do not invent titles, affiliations, dates, publication status, availability, or contact information.

## CV summary or bullet

Start with the system, result, or responsibility. Use active verbs and include scale, validation, adoption, or impact only when sourced. Keep the bullet compact and distinguish individual contribution from team outcomes.
